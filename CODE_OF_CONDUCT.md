# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Keep technical criticism focused on the work.
- Assume maintainers and contributors are trying to improve the project.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks and exclusionary behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Threats or intimidation.
- Harassment, slurs or discriminatory comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Disruptive behavior that blocks normal project work.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through the channels in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). The
maintainer reviews reports and takes action appropriate to their severity and
context.
